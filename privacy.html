Privacy information

Your projects stay yours.

This privacy policy describes Row Counter: Offline Knit, an Android app with a connected Wear OS companion, published under the developer brand Lambda Castle.

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Data on your device

Projects, counters, history, patterns, attachments, inventory, sessions and settings are stored locally. Core features work offline. There is no app account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated cloud/backend. Uninstalling or clearing storage deletes local data, attachments, trash and recovery points. Android automatic backup and device transfer are excluded.

Connections and optional access

The phone requests Internet access. When you choose web, Ravelry or video links, the external browser or provider handles those visits, sign-in and any purchases under its own policies. User-directed HTTPS pattern downloads are bounded; saved HTML is inert and viewed offline. Servers can receive ordinary connection information. The paired watch exchanges counter data with the phone using Google Play services; it is not a standalone app.

Optional voice input requests microphone access and requires supported on-device recognition; spoken output uses an installed text-to-speech service. The optional floating counter requires overlay approval and an enabled foreground service notification, including notification permission on supported Android versions. A home-screen widget is optional. These platform services have their own privacy practices.

Backups, recovery and trash

Export a full v2 ZIP with attachments, history, undo and trash through the Android document picker; selected CSV exports are for inspection, not full restore. A legacy v1 JSON backup can also be imported. ZIP limits are 256 MiB uncompressed in total, 25 MiB per attachment and 5 MiB for core JSON; legacy JSON is limited to 5 MiB.

Restoring replaces local data after preview and confirmation and creates a dated local recovery point first. You can also create, restore or delete recovery points. Deleted projects enter 30-day trash and may be restored or permanently deleted; expiry is enforced when trash is listed. Local recovery and trash are lost on uninstall or storage clearing. Export a separate backup first if you need one.

Exported files can remain after uninstall, and anyone with access can read them. The document provider you choose may sync or process files under its own policies; protect your exports.

Google Play

If the app is distributed through Google Play, Google processes purchases and may process account, payment and platform information under its own terms and privacy practices. The developer's app does not process your payment information.
